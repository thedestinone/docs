For media inquiries, assignments, access questions, or coverage requests, email is the fastest way to get a response.

  </Card>

  </Card>

## Email

Send inquiries to the assignment desk at the address published on the [main site](https://north-texas-sports-photo.mintlify.app).

## Response times

<Note>
  Standard response time is within two business days. Same-week assignment requests are handled on a best-effort basis; game-day emergencies are best sent by phone if a number was exchanged during booking.
</Note>
