North Texas Sports is an independent digital publication covering high-school athletics in the North Texas area. The publication is built around original photography and editorial game coverage, including photo galleries, game-day features, team stories, and event photography.

## The approach

Game coverage should feel like being there: the action, emotion, preparation, and people that make a night memorable. Every assignment aims for consistent, respectful, editorial coverage that gives athletes, teams, and communities a lasting visual record of their season.

## What "independent" means

North Texas Sports is not affiliated with any school district, athletic conference, or media network. Coverage decisions are editorial, and access is coordinated directly with schools and event organizers on a per-assignment basis.
