Every covered game or event is published as a gallery on the North Texas Sports site. Galleries are organized by matchup and date so athletes, families, and schools can find their photos quickly.

## How galleries are organized

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="By matchup">
    Named for the two teams, sport & tier, and the date.
  </Card>

  <Card title="By season">
    All galleries from a school year are grouped under that season.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Featured coverage">
    Highlighted matchups appear on the front page as the latest coverage.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
