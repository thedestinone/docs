Not every frame from a game makes it into the published gallery. If you're looking for a specific player, play, or moment, submit a photo request and it can often be pulled from the unpublished set.

## What you can request

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Specific players">
    All available photos of a jersey number or named player from a covered game.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Specific plays">
    A particular touchdown, catch, or moment you remember from the game.
  </Card>

  <Card title="High-resolution files">
    Full-resolution versions of gallery photos for personal or licensed use.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to submit a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the game">
    Note the matchup, date, and gallery URL if the game is already published.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you need">
    Player name and jersey number, quarter or approximate game time, or a link to the gallery photo closest to what you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Email the details from the [Contact](/contact) page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turnaround

Photo requests are typically handled within a few days. Higher-volume requests around playoffs may take longer.

<Tip>
  The more specific the request, the faster it can be filled. A jersey number and quarter is much easier to work with than "any photos of my son."
</Tip>
