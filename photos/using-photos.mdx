Photos published by North Texas Sports Media are original work and remain the property of the publication. Different uses have different rules, and this page explains what's allowed and how to request licensing when needed.

## Personal use

Athletes, families, and friends are welcome to share and repost gallery photos for personal, non-commercial use.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Allowed">
    Sharing to personal social media, printing for home, sending to family.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Please credit">
    Tag or credit _North Texas Sports_ when reposting online.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Team, school, and booster use

Schools, teams, and booster clubs may use photos for non-commercial team purposes such as social media, banquets, and season recaps, with credit.

<Note>
  Use in fundraising materials, paid advertising, or merchandise requires a separate license. Contact the assignment desk to arrange.
</Note>

## Media and publication licensing

Local outlets, team sites, and publications wanting to republish photos should request a media license. Include:

- The photo or gallery URL.
- Where the photo will be used (article URL, print edition, etc.).
- Publication date.

## What's not allowed

<Warning>
  Photos cannot be resold, used in paid advertising, or altered to remove credit or watermarks without written permission.
</Warning>

For anything outside personal use, reach out through the [Contact](/contact) page to confirm terms.
