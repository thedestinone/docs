Quick answers to the questions that come up most often from schools, athletes, families, and publications.

## Coverage

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do you charge for coverage?">
    Coverage terms vary by assignment type (editorial coverage, school-booked shoots, event photography). Reach out through the [Contact](/contact) page for specifics.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What areas does North Texas Sports Media cover?">
    Coverage is focused on high-school athletics across the North Texas region. Travel outside the region is considered case by case.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can you cover multiple games in one night?">
    Occasionally, if venues and start times allow. Note preferred alternates when you submit the request.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Photos and galleries

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When are galleries published?">
    Typically within 48 hours of the final whistle. Playoff weeks and multi-game weekends may take slightly longer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see myself in the gallery. Can I request specific photos?">
    Yes. Not every frame is published. See [Photo requests](/photos/photo-requests) for how to ask for a specific player, play, or moment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use these photos on my social media?">
    Yes for personal use, with credit to _North Texas Sports_. See [Using photos](/photos/using-photos) for team, school, and media use.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get high-resolution downloads?">
    Yes. Submit a [photo request](/photos/photo-requests) with the gallery link and what you'd like.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Media and press

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can our publication republish a photo?">
    Yes, with a media license. See [Using photos](/photos/using-photos) for how to request one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are you affiliated with a school or district?">
    No. North Texas Sports is an independent publication. Coverage decisions are editorial.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
