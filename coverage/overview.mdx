North Texas Sports Media covers high-school athletics across the region, with a primary focus on football during the fall season and additional sports throughout the school year. This page explains what's covered and what to expect from an assignment.

## What to expect from an assignment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pre-game coordination">
    Confirmation of date, location, teams, and any credential or sideline access required by the host school.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On-site coverage">
    Sideline and field-level photography during the game, with attention to both action and the surrounding moments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-game edit">
    A curated gallery of edited photos, typically delivered within 48 hours of the final whistle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How assignments are prioritized

Because North Texas Sports is a small independent operation, not every request can be accepted. Priority generally goes to:

- Games submitted well in advance of the season.
- Matchups with regional or playoff significance.
- Schools and programs with established coverage history.

<Note>
  Even if a specific date can't be covered, submit the request. Alternative dates or nearby games in the same week are often available.
</Note>
