Once an assignment is confirmed, the game-day workflow follows a consistent process from pre-game arrival through gallery delivery. This page walks through what happens at each stage.

## Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pre-game (60 to 90 minutes before kickoff)">
    Arrival at the venue, check-in with the host school's media contact, credential pickup if required, and sideline setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Warm-ups">
    Team arrival, pre-game rituals, and warm-up shots. This is often where portrait-style and editorial frames come from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Game action">
    Sideline and end-zone coverage throughout the game, balancing action, reactions, and sideline moments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-game">
    Handshakes, celebrations, and any senior-night or milestone moments as the game wraps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and publish">
    Selects are edited overnight. The finished gallery is typically published within 48 hours of the final whistle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What schools should prepare

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Media check-in" icon="id-card">
    Confirm where to check in on arrival and who from the school will meet the photographer.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sideline access" icon="location-arrow">
    Sideline or field-level credentials, if required by district or venue policy.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Roster info" icon="clipboard-list">
    A current roster with numbers helps ensure accurate captions and player identification.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Point of contact" icon="phone">
    A phone number for the on-site AD or coach in case of last-minute schedule changes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Weather delays and lightning holds are common in the fall. Coverage resumes with the game whenever play restarts.
</Tip>
