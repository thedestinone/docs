Whether you're a parent or a player looking for photos after one, this quickstart gets you from request to result.

## Find your game photos

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the gallery index">
    Visit the [photo galleries](/photos/galleries) page to browse by date, team, or event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate your game">
    Galleries are labeled by matchup and date (for example, _Ryan vs. Lewisville, September 11, 2026_).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request downloads or licensing">
    See [Using photos](/photos/using-photos) for personal use, team use, and media licensing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Coaches and athletic directors: send your full season schedule at the start of the year so games can be prioritized ahead of time.
</Tip>
